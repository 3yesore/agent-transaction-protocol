# Semantic Amendment Under a Quorum

Generated by experiments/meta-consensus/run.ts.

ATP-0002's one rule is that a transition is evaluated under the semantics in force
BEFORE it, so an amendment R0 -> R1 must be authorised by R0. On a chain that means a
quorum selects it. This measures what changes when the thing being selected is the
rules themselves.

An object-level transition is one of a stream. A semantic amendment is a single,
self-replacing, irreversible event. Three asymmetries follow.

## 1. Frequency

A per-round shared cause is averaged away over a stream. Over N amendments it is not,
because the question is whether it coincides with ANY of them:

~~~text
P(at least one capture) = 1 - (1 - p)^N
~~~

Correlated: one client family, capture rate == the shared-cause rate. Independent: no
shared cause at all, so capture needs 18 of 35 validators to flip on their own.

| amendments N | correlated (rho = 0.2) | independent (V=35, k=18, p=0.15) |
|--------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 1 | 20.0% | 0.0001% |
| 3 | 48.8% | 0.0002% |
| 5 | 67.2% | 0.0003% |
| 10 | 89.3% | 0.0005% |
| 21 | 99.1% | 0.0011% |
| 50 | 100.0% | 0.0025% |

## 2. Persistence

An object-level bad state is reversible under unchanged rules. A captured amendment
replaces the rules, so nothing in the new semantics is obliged to offer a way back.

| N | object-level bad rounds | meta-level bad rounds | meta captures | ratio |
|---|-------------------------|-----------------------|---------------|-------|
| 10 | 17.9% | 64.4% | 89.3% | 3.6x |
| 50 | 18.2% | 92.1% | 100.0% | 5.1x |
| 100 | 18.2% | 96.0% | 100.0% | 5.3x |
| 400 | 18.2% | 99.0% | 100.0% | 5.4x |

## 3. Divergence

Diversity lowers the object-level floor, because it de-correlates failures. It raises
the chance that implementations read R0 differently, and at the meta level a
disagreement about the rules is not a rejected transition - it is two rule sets.

~~~text
P(fork) = 1 - (1 - d)^m
~~~

35 validators, majority threshold, rho = 0.2, d = 0.02 per implementation.

| implementations m | family split | object-level floor | P(meta fork) | loss, severity 1 | loss, severity 10 |
|-------------------|--------------|--------------------|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 35 | 20.0% | 2.0% | 0.220 | 0.400 |
| 2 | 18+17 | 20.0% | 4.0% | 0.240 | 0.596 |
| 3 | 12+12+11 | 10.4% | 5.9% | 0.163 | 0.692 |
| 5 | 7+7+7+7+7 | 5.8% | 9.6% | 0.154 | 1.019 |

## Conclusion

**1. Frequency turns a tolerable per-round rate into a near-certain lifetime event.**
A shared cause at 0.2 that a streaming system averages away gives a 50% chance of
capturing an amendment within three attempts and 99% within twenty-one. The chain does
not need the cause to persist; it only needs it to coincide.

**2. Persistence makes the damage unbounded rather than bounded.** At the object level
the bad-round fraction converges on p/(p+c) - about 18% here - because a later
transition under unchanged rules can correct it. At the meta level capture is
absorbing and the fraction converges on 100%. The same per-event rate, roughly five
times the lifetime damage, growing with the protocol's age.

**3. Diversity is a safety win at the object level and a fork risk at the meta level.**
The floor falls as implementations are added and the chance that they read the rules
differently rises. There is no m that minimises both.

**4. The design conclusion is uncomfortable but clear.** If a fork of the rules is
expensive - and it should be, since it splits the chain's common ground - the optimum
is a SINGLE implementation with the effort put into making that implementation correct,
not into validator count or diversity. A self-amending chain can have self-amendment or
implementation diversity, and it cannot have both.

That is a boundary result against the naive plan. It says the interesting engineering
problem is not the consensus mechanism; it is reducing the shared-cause rate of one
implementation, plus designing an amendment procedure unambiguous enough that
independent readers cannot diverge on it.
